FAQ

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Everything you need to know to get started. Still wondering something? Talk to our team ↗

What does a dispatcher do?+

We help carriers find suitable freight, communicate with brokers, negotiate rates, coordinate load details, manage paperwork and provide ongoing operational support.

Do you work with owner-operators?+

Yes. Our service is designed for owner-operators as well as small fleets.

Do I have to accept the loads you find?+

No. We present available opportunities, and you remain in control of which loads you accept.

How do you find loads?+

We use freight marketplaces and load boards, combined with our workflows and technology, to identify opportunities that fit your operation.

Do you negotiate with brokers?+

Yes. We communicate directly with brokers regarding available loads and negotiate rates and load details on your behalf.

What happens if something goes wrong?+

If a truck breaks down, a pickup is delayed, an appointment changes or another issue comes up, we help communicate with the relevant parties and coordinate next steps.

Do you use AI?+

We use AI-assisted systems and automation to streamline repetitive work, organize information and improve internal workflows. AI supports our dispatchers — it doesn't replace them.

What is your dispatch fee?+

We use a percentage-based dispatch fee tied to the revenue generated from loads we dispatch for your operation. Exact terms are provided in the carrier agreement.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?+

Contract terms depend on your carrier agreement. We'll walk you through the agreement and answer your questions before you sign.

What equipment do you work with?+

We can support dry vans, reefers, flatbeds and other equipment depending on your operation and available freight.
