Quick answers

Good to know.

Still have a question? Our team is happy to help.

Talk to our team
Does AI choose my loads?

No. AI helps organize and surface options. Your dispatcher reviews them, and you decide what to accept.

Who will I communicate with?

A real dispatcher. Technology helps our team stay organized; people handle communication and decisions.

What equipment do you support?

We work with dry vans, reefers, flatbeds and other equipment depending on your operation.

How does pricing work?

Our dispatch fee is percentage-based on revenue from loads we dispatch: 0% during your 14-day free trial, 4% for your first paid month, then 8%. Exact terms are provided in the carrier agreement.

What do you ask in return for the free trial?

We ask you to share an honest testimonial or satisfaction review about your experience. It helps us improve and gives other carriers a clearer picture of our service.
