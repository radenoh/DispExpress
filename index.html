Still have a question? Talk to our team ↗

Does AI choose my loads?+

No. AI helps organize and surface options. Your dispatcher reviews them, and you decide what to accept.

Who will I communicate with?+

A real dispatcher. Technology helps our team stay organized; people handle communication and decisions.

What equipment do you support?+

We work with dry vans, reefers, flatbeds and other equipment depending on your operation.

How does pricing work?+

Our dispatch fee is percentage-based on revenue from loads we dispatch. Exact terms are provided in the carrier agreement.

How do I get started?+

Send your carrier details below. Our team will follow up about your truck and lanes.
